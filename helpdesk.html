A useful request

Five details help us answer faster.

No formal template is required. A short, concrete example is often the best starting point.

  1. 01
    Your goal

    Describe the research question or problem you are trying to solve.

  2. 02
    The resource

    Name the language, wordnet or lexical dataset involved.

  3. 03
    Your setup

    Include the relevant format, tool and version.

  4. 04
    A small example

    Share a minimal sample and the exact error message, if applicable.

  5. 05
    Useful links

    Add public data or documentation where available.
