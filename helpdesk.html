What to include

There is no form to fill in. These details help us answer faster, and a short, concrete example is often the best place to start.

  • Your goalThe research question or problem you are trying to solve.
  • The resourceThe language, wordnet or lexical dataset involved.
  • Your setupThe format, tool and version you are using.
  • A small exampleA minimal sample and the exact error message, if there is one.
  • Useful linksPublic data or documentation, where available.

Please don't send confidential or personal data by email.
