Using this website

The website uses semantic headings and landmarks, visible keyboard focus, a skip link, layouts that adapt to small screens, text alternatives for meaningful images, and your system's reduced-motion setting. All information and the helpdesk email address are available without JavaScript.

Known limitations

Linked resources are run by other organisations and may be less accessible. When certification is complete, the certificate image will come with a direct link to the document and a text description.

Report a problem

If any part of this website is hard to use, email {{ site.contact_email }}. Please tell us the page address, the problem, and the browser or assistive technology you were using.

This statement was prepared on 17 September 2026 and will be reviewed once the site moves to its final address and certification is complete.
