Using this website

The website uses semantic headings and landmarks, keyboard-visible focus styles, a skip link, responsive layouts, text alternatives for meaningful images and reduced-motion preferences. Core information and email contact remain available without client-side JavaScript.

Known limitations

Linked third-party resources are operated by other organisations and may have different accessibility levels. The visual certificate will be accompanied by a direct document link and descriptive information when certification is complete.

Report a problem

If any part of this website is difficult to use, email {{ site.contact_email }}. Please include the page address, the problem you encountered, and the browser or assistive technology you used.

This statement was prepared on 17 September 2026 and will be reviewed after the production domain and final certification content are published.
