What can the helpdesk advise on?

Wordnet design, sense and synset modelling, semantic relations, formats, validation, multilingual alignment, publication, and using wordnets in research or software.

Do you support wordnets for languages other than Polish or English?

Yes. The centre works on methods and standards that apply across languages. Tell us the language and any language-specific requirements in your message so we can find the most relevant expertise.

Which exchange format should a new wordnet use?

For interoperable publication, start with the current Global WordNet Formats. The best choice can depend on your workflow and the services you want to use, so contact us before converting a mature resource.

Can you help align a wordnet across languages?

Yes. We can discuss concept mapping, alignment strategies, shared identifiers such as the Collaborative Interlingual Index, evidence, quality control and publishing in a multilingual setting.

Can the centre build or annotate a whole resource for my project?

The helpdesk gives advice and technical guidance. Larger development, annotation or evaluation work depends on scope and capacity, and needs a separate collaboration agreement.

What should I send with a technical problem?

A small example that reproduces the problem, the format and tool version, what you expected, what happened instead, and the exact error message. Please don't send confidential or personal data by email.

How quickly will I get a reply?

We aim to acknowledge messages within two working days. A full answer can take longer if a question needs specialist review.

Still have a question?

Tell us about the resource, the language, your goal and what is getting in the way.
