What can the helpdesk advise on?

We support questions about wordnet design, sense and synset modelling, semantic relations, formats, validation, multilingual alignment, publication and research or software use.

Do you support wordnets for languages other than Polish or English?

Yes. The centre focuses on methods and standards that apply across languages. Include the language and any language-specific requirements in your enquiry so that we can identify the most relevant expertise.

Which exchange format should a new wordnet use?

For interoperable publication, begin with the current schemas maintained by the Global WordNet Association. The best choice can depend on your workflow and target services, so contact us before converting a mature resource.

Can you help align a wordnet across languages?

Yes. We can discuss concept mapping, alignment strategies, shared identifiers, evidence, quality control and publication in a multilingual setting.

Can the centre build or annotate an entire resource for my project?

The helpdesk provides consultation and technical guidance. Larger development, annotation or evaluation work depends on scope, capacity and a separate collaboration agreement.

What should I send with a technical problem?

Include a small reproducible example, the format and tool version, the expected result, the actual result and the exact error message. Avoid sending confidential or personally identifiable data by email.

How quickly will I receive a response?

We aim to acknowledge enquiries within two working days. A complete answer may take longer when a question requires specialist review.

Still unsure?

Ask a focused question.

Describe the resource, language, goal and current obstacle. We will route the enquiry to the right expertise.
