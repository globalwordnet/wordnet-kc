What we do

From a research question
to a connected resource.

The Wordnet Knowledge Centre initiative brings together expertise in lexical resources, semantic networks and multilingual language technology.

We support the full life cycle of a wordnet: planning its structure, selecting standards, building and validating data, aligning languages and applying the resource in research or software.

01

Expert consultation

Focused advice for research design, data modelling and practical use of wordnets.

02

Technical guidance

Support with formats, validation, alignment workflows and interoperability.

03

Training and knowledge sharing

Workshops and practical materials for researchers, lexicographers and developers.
