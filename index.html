About Our Wordnet K-Centre

The Wordnet CLARIN Knowledge Centre specializes in providing expertise and support for wordnet technologies, lexical resources, and semantic networks. As a CLARIN K-Centre, we are dedicated to promoting knowledge about wordnet infrastructure and supporting researchers in their semantic analysis projects.

Our centre offers comprehensive support for wordnet creation, management, and application across various languages and domains. We provide expert guidance on lexical semantic analysis, ontology development, and cross-lingual wordnet alignment within Digital Humanities and Social Sciences research.
