About the centre

Wordnet-KC is a CLARIN Knowledge Centre: a place researchers can come to for advice on wordnets and lexical semantics. We help people build, use and link wordnets for research in linguistics, the digital humanities and the social sciences.

The centre brings together people from CLARIN-PL, the Open Multilingual Wordnet and the Global Wordnet Association.

What is a wordnet?

A wordnet is a dictionary organised by meaning. Words that share a sense are grouped into synsets (sets of synonyms), and each synset has a short definition. Synsets are linked by relations such as hypernymy (a car is a kind of motor vehicle), meronymy (a car door is part of a car) and antonymy (hot and cold).

Princeton WordNet (Miller 1995; Fellbaum 1998) set the model for English. Wordnets have since been built for dozens of languages, and many are linked through the Collaborative Interlingual Index (CILI), so you can follow a concept from one language to another.
